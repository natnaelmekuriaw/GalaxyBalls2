# Ludo

Choose **You vs 1–3 CPU** or **2–4 players on this device**. Local players pass the same device after each turn. The current match saves after every move; open Ludo again and tap **Resume match**.

## Play

1. Tap **Roll the die**.
2. A **six** releases a piece from its yard. Tap a glowing piece on the board or its numbered button below.
3. Move around the shared track and then up your color's home lane. The eight star and start squares are safe from capture.
4. Landing on an opponent on an unmarked track square sends that piece back to its yard.
5. A six, capture, or piece reaching the center gives another turn. Three consecutive sixes end the turn.
6. Reach the center with an exact roll. The first player to bring all four pieces home wins.

The computer chooses among legal moves, favoring captures, finishes, safe squares, and releasing new pieces. The app supports English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromoo. Starting a new match asks before replacing an unfinished one.
