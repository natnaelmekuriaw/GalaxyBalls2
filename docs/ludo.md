# Ludo

Pick 2–6 seats with any mix of people and computers (at least one person). Five or six seats use the six-arm board. Local players share the device.

| Choice | Available options |
| --- | --- |
| Board | Classic (default), Jewel, Garden, Pastel |
| Audio | Original looping music and separate sound effects |
| Saving | Match, each die result, board style, setup, and audio choices |

Tap the die beside your color to roll, then a highlighted piece or its numbered button. A six releases a piece. Stars are safe. Captures, sixes, and finishing a piece give another turn; three sixes lose the turn. Bring all four pieces home with exact rolls to win.

Open the game menu to continue, change the board or audio, or choose a new match. Replacing an unfinished match asks first. Progress saves after every turn. The game pauses computer turns and audio when it leaves the foreground.

English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromoo are supported. Music starts after an interaction on browsers. Audio is bundled and needs no network access.
